How these numbers are calculated
  • Kept counts AI words and the person's story prose on the branch that was selected when the chat was exported. Some AI words may be planning or feedback, so treat it as the most that could have reached the manuscript.
  • Story prose and instructions are sorted automatically. Text in ((double parentheses)), [square brackets], {{double braces}}, or after labels such as OOC: counts as instruction. Unmarked paragraphs are judged by their wording, and uncertain ones are flagged for review. Open a message to correct all of its guessed text, or click one passage to switch it. Corrections are remembered in this browser.
  • Advanced sorting follows these rules. A switched passage always wins. Choosing Prose, Instruction, or Story for a message sets its guessed text and lifts any word exclusion on it, while HTML tag choices and explicit markers stay in place. Choosing Continuity applies to the whole message. Otherwise, excluded words apply first, then tags and markers, then the automatic sorting. Tag markup itself is not counted as prose or instruction.
  • Multiple chat logs are combined. A chat found in two files is counted once using its fullest copy, and messages shared by copied chats are counted once.
  • Abandoned counts every other AI word and story-prose word: regenerated replies and branches the person left. Instructions are never counted as manuscript.
  • Continuity notes are summaries, handoffs, and background records. They are kept out of the manuscript, AI output, the person's prose, and direction totals.
  • Repeated text is any run of 40 or more identical words already seen in an earlier message, ignoring capitalization and punctuation. Only the first appearance counts, so a summary pasted into a new chat is not counted again. Text on a kept branch takes priority over an abandoned copy.
  • Hours worked adds the time between consecutive messages, using the export's timestamps. Any pause longer than the break setting counts as time away. AI generation time is subtracted when the export records it; otherwise the total includes waiting for the AI. Time around messages that are mostly continuity notes or repeated text is left out unless you choose to count it. Work done outside the chat is not included.
  • Reading time assumes 238 words per minute, a typical adult silent reading speed. It shows how long reading every generated word once would take, whether or not the person read all of it.
  • Attempts rejected counts each extra branch at a fork. Three replies to one prompt counts as two rejected attempts.
  • Retried prompts count only the words that changed. A prompt is treated as a retry when at least 60% of its words match, in order, an earlier version beside it or one of the three prompts before it on the same branch. The kept version receives credit for the shared words, and abandoned versions count only what was unique to them. The retry still counts as an attempt, and its time still counts as work.
  • Discarded words above, for a single message, adds all words at earlier turns that are not on that message's own path. Other attempts at the same turn are not included.
  • Shared dashboards contain only totals, weekly hours, node shapes, categories, and rough lengths. They are self-reported and are not independently verified.
  • Hidden model reasoning and tool output stored in collapsible blocks is left out of every count. Your file is read in this browser and is not uploaded.
